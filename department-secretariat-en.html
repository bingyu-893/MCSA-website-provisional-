Join us behind the scenes and help make MCSA happen. Make more of your university experience!

The Secretariat supports MCSA through event preparation and delivery, information sharing and coordination between departments. We are a point of contact for the wider community, a bridge for internal communication and a team working behind every event.

Messages from our leaders

Our core teams

External team

At the heart of the event

01

Before the event, prepare the hosting and management schedule.

02

Attend rehearsals or meetings to confirm equipment and staffing, helping everything run smoothly.

03

During the event, take on roles such as host, scorekeeper or referee.

04

Afterwards, help review the event and collect feedback to improve the next one.

Hosting · Floor management · Event coordination · Communication

Internal team

Friendly support behind the scenes

01

Answer new students’ questions, share useful benefits and information, support clear communication and maintain a welcoming community.

02

Coordinate day-to-day communication and arrangements, following the details and key requirements throughout an event.

03

During preparation, organise supplies, venues and setup, and coordinate check-in records and staffing.

Assistant accounts · Internal coordination · Care · Communication

Information Technology team

Building MCSA’s online platforms

01

Build, develop and maintain MCSA’s online platforms, update event information and improve useful features to provide convenient online services.

02

Keep the website and mini program running reliably, improve features and resolve technical issues to create a better user experience.

03

Provide technical support through website and mini program development, ongoing maintenance and feature updates.

Platform building · Development · Learning · Technical support

Roles and requirements

External team

Responsibilities

  • Hosting
  • Refereeing
  • Check-in and ticket checking

Requirements

  • Fluent Mandarin
  • Outgoing and energetic
  • Hosting experience is a plus

Internal team

Responsibilities

  • Manage assistant accounts, share internal event information and coordinate communication with new students and members so information reaches the right people.
  • Support large events through venue setup, check-in and practical delivery tasks.

Requirements

  • Patient, enthusiastic and willing to help—no previous experience required!
  • Service-minded and empathetic, with patience, attention to detail and good listening skills
  • Calm and positive when responding to new students’ questions
  • Responsible, considerate in communication and a strong team player

Information Technology team

The original round advertised 10 places (past recruitment).

Website team

Develop, maintain and improve the MCSA website through full-stack development, expanding its features and making it easier to use.

  • Undergraduate level or above; preference for students in IT, CS, fine arts or visual design
  • Basic programming skills or a strong interest in programming preferred
  • Interest in full-stack development preferred
  • Good communication and teamwork

Mini program team

Develop and maintain the MCSA WeChat mini program, including new features, bug fixes and releases.

  • Undergraduate level or above; preference for students in IT, CS, fine arts or visual design
  • Interest in mini program development (WXML/WXSS/JS/TS)
  • Willingness to spend time learning and contribute consistently
  • Good communication and teamwork
We value your ability to learn, sense of responsibility and continued commitment. It is fine if you are new to this—as long as you are willing to learn.
